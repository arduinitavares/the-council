Missing matching qualification is a reason to enter the explicit bounded first-use procedure once its bootstrap prerequisites are satisfied; it is not permission for an arbitrary production dispatch. Explicit unknown allowance or optional unexposed telemetry is not by itself an exhaustion/denial. Missing required controls, authentication restrictions, actual denials, and unsupported exact selections remain specific blockers. Unknown balance never authorizes new spending or changes the permitted billing source.

**Key interfaces:**

- Supported Council skill/launcher: separates installed-package resolution from requesting workspace identity/cwd and owns the progress path from inspection through qualification, dispatch, collection, and settlement.
- `ModelSelection` and council configuration validation: accept the E2 exact three-voter roster plus nonvoting judge, including the Cursor model-encoded effort distinction, without silently changing another saved roster.
- `runPreflight()` / `preflightInputSchema`: retain explicit evidence states and produce actionable admission/blocker results. Each blocker identifies the affected route/control, observed fact or absence, evidence source, and next permitted action.
- `qualifySelection()` / qualification receipts: match host, relevant versions, model/effort, action scope, workspace/roots and relevant controls. Reuse only matching valid proof; record normal and cancellation outcomes and settled resources for new proof.
- Native execution adapter or supervised pilot: uses supported Orca launch/observe/stop/release operations, verifies requested versus exposed identity, and binds results to the owned attempt. `SyntheticDriver` remains a test seam, never evidence of native success.
- Outcome/evidence record: separately reports preflight disposition, qualification attempts, topic attempts, model/route selections, collected results, decision outcome, and resource settlement. Preserve explicit unknown observations; do not infer completion from an idle screen or exit zero alone.
